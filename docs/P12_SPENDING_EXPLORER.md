# P12 — Spending Explorer & Interactive Drill-Down Analytics\n\nP12 turns the P11 analytics engine into an interactive spending investigation surface.\n\n## Drill-down model\n\n~~~text\nAll spending\n→ category\n→ subcategory\n→ merchant / necessity / product\n→ individual product purchase evidence\n→ Activity\n~~~\n\nThe accounting boundary is strict: category and merchant totals come from the posted ledger. Product amounts are explanatory receipt-item evidence and never replace or duplicate ledger spending.\n\n## Ledger truth\n\nLedger-derived values include gross spending, refunds/reimbursements, net spending, transaction count, category hierarchy totals, merchant totals, necessity totals, scoped time series, and previous-period comparisons. Transfers remain excluded.\n\n## Product evidence\n\nProduct detail is included only from confirmed receipts, confirmed receipt-to-transaction matches, posted matched transactions, non-excluded receipt items, and normalized product IDs. Bank expenses without a confirmed itemized receipt remain in ledger totals but do not fabricate product detail.\n\nAn itemized coverage ratio states how much scoped ledger spending is represented by confirmed receipt items.\n\n## Recursive categories\n\nThe backend recursively resolves category descendants. Selecting Food & Drink includes all descendant ledger entries, while the response exposes only immediate children as the next drill layer. Breadcrumbs are generated from the selected node to its ancestors.\n\n## Filters\n\nPeriod, category subtree, merchant, and necessity filters apply consistently to summary, time series, category children, merchants, necessity mix, and product evidence.\n\nSupported necessity values are essential, flexible, discretionary, and unclassified.\n\n## Periods\n\nWeek, Month, Quarter, and Year use Finance-profile timezone boundaries. Default bucket grouping is Week → Day, Month → Day, Quarter → Week, Year → Month.\n\n## Refund semantics\n\nRefund-only time-series buckets remain negative. A €100 expense followed by a €30 refund produces €100 then −€30, and the series sums back to the €70 scoped summary.\n\n## Backend API\n\n~~~text\nfinance.get_spending_explorer(...)\npublic.finance_get_spending_explorer(...)\n\nfinance.get_spending_explorer_period(...)\npublic.finance_get_spending_explorer_period(...)\n\nfinance.get_product_purchase_evidence(...)\npublic.finance_get_product_purchase_evidence(...)\n~~~\n\nAll RPCs are SECURITY INVOKER. Public and anonymous execute permission is revoked; authenticated users and service role receive explicit execute permission. RLS remains the ownership boundary.\n\n## Application files\n\n~~~text\nsrc/domain/spending-explorer.ts\nsrc/services/spending-explorer.ts\nsrc/services/supabase-spending-explorer.ts\n\nsrc/spending-explorer/SpendingExplorerPage.tsx\nsrc/spending-explorer/use-spending-explorer.ts\nsrc/spending-explorer/spending-explorer.css\nsrc/spending-explorer/supabase-spending-explorer.test.ts\n~~~\n\nThe shared browser runtime exposes runtime.spendingExplorer.\n\n## UI\n\nThe live explorer is available from Insights and Categories. It provides period controls, recursive breadcrumbs, necessity chips, merchant filtering, ledger summary cards, current-vs-previous trend chart/table, category composition, necessity composition, merchant ranking bars, product evidence ranking, inline individual-purchase evidence, and Activity handoff.\n\n## Live verification\n\nThe main rollback-only fixture verifies Food & Drink → Snacks → Chocolate recursion, Groceries sibling roll-up, REWE and Kiosk merchant amounts after refund adjustment, discretionary classification, merchant-filtered Food totals, confirmed product evidence, individual purchase evidence, and cross-user RLS isolation.\n\nA separate fixture verifies negative refund buckets and exact series-to-summary reconciliation.\n\n## Advisors\n\n~~~text\nSupabase Security advisor     clean\nSupabase Performance advisor  clean\n~~~\n\n## P13 handoff\n\nP13 can deepen product intelligence with product/family history, purchase frequency, price history, merchant comparison, cheapest observed merchant, quantity trends, and price-change detection while reusing P12 confirmed receipt-item evidence.
